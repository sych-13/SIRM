DATA MODEL AND SECURITY CONTEXT

Teacher queries require `teacher_uids` to contain the teacher Auth UID. Student queries require `owner_uid` to equal the student's Auth UID. Never store passwords in Firestore. Do not expose student data or grant broad collection access to make screens easier to implement.
